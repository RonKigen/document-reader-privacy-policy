Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Document Reader ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles your information when you use our app.

1. Information Collection and Storage

Our app operates locally on your device:

  • Local File Access: Document Reader requests permission to access your device's storage solely to allow you to select, open, and view documents (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint) stored locally on your device.
  • No Data Upload: We do not collect, upload, store, or transmit your personal documents, files, or data to any external servers or cloud storage. All file parsing and rendering happen entirely offline on your device.

2. Permissions Used by the App

To provide core functionality, the app may request the following permissions:

  • Storage / Media Permissions: Required to locate and read document files requested by you.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not integrate third-party analytics trackers, advertising SDKs, or external databases that harvest your personal information.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Since all document processing happens locally and offline, no data is intentionally gathered from any user.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
